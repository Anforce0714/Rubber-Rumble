🧽 Rubber Rumble

**Aim. Charge. Cover.**

Rubber Rumble is a casual browser-based arcade battle game where two erasers compete on a tabletop arena. Aim your shot, build up your launching power, and send your eraser flying towards your opponent.

Your objective is simple: **completely cover your opponent's eraser to win!**

🎮 **Play Now:** [Rubber Rumble](https://anforce0714.github.io/Rubber-Rumble/)

---

## 🎯 Gameplay

- 🔵 **Player vs AI** – Challenge an AI opponent.
- 🎯 **Aim Your Shot** – Choose the direction of your attack.
- ⚡ **Charge Your Power** – Hold the launch button to build up power.
- 💥 **Launch & Bounce** – Send your eraser across the tabletop arena.
- 🥊 **Arena Boundaries** – Keep both erasers inside the playing area.
- 🏆 **Cover to Win** – Completely cover the opponent's eraser to claim victory.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Click or tap the tabletop to select your shooting direction.
2. Hold the launch button to charge your power.
3. Release to launch your eraser.
4. Watch your opponent's movement and plan your next shot.
5. Cover the opponent's eraser completely to win!
