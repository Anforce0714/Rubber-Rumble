HOW TO PLAY

  1. Aim. Click or tap the table to point your eraser. You can drag to fine-tune the direction.
  2. Charge. Press and hold the shoot button. The power bar fills up. Don't overshoot!
  3. Shoot. Release the button to launch your eraser through the air.
  4. Cover. Land your eraser on top of the red one so it is fully covered. Match its angle too!
  5. Take turns. If you miss, the AI shoots back. If it covers your eraser first, you lose.
  6. Progress. Win a level to unlock the next one. Beat Easy Level 20 to unlock Hard Mode.
